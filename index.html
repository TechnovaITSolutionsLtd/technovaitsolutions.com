The game loop

One round. Three clear beats.

Mind the Cards is built around recall rather than matching. Nothing stays face-up to help you.

  1. 01◉

    Watch

    Cards appear one at a time. Notice both the picture and its position.

  2. 02✦

    Remember

    The sequence disappears. Keep the order in mind while the tray arrives.

  3. 03↺

    Rebuild

    Tap the cards in sequence, check your answer and review every position.
