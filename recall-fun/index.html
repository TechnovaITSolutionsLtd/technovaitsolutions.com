How the game works

  1. Choose a ready-made challenge.
  2. Watch the cards appear one at a time.
  3. Remember their exact order.
  4. Tap the same cards in sequence to rebuild the answer.
  5. Review the result and track your progress.

Ready-made challenges have fixed rules, so results are comparable. The included free games provide the complete play loop. Pro unlocks the full ready-made collection and detailed statistics.

Classic Climb and platform gaming

Classic Climb begins with three cards and adds one card after each perfect round. The run ends on the first mistake. Eligible Classic Climb scores and achievement milestones can be submitted to Apple Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games Services on Android. Custom games do not contribute to the competitive leaderboard.

Game Designer

Game Designer lets you build and save custom challenges using installed card sets. You can set a sequence from 3 to 100 cards, card display speed, countdown duration, repeated-card rules, label visibility and entire-deck mode. A saved design stores its rules, not a fixed answer; every round generates a new sequence.

Support

Where can I find the exact version?

Open the Help tab in the app. The exact app version and build number appear at the bottom of that screen.

Where are my progress and custom games?

Statistics, settings, favourites and saved designs are stored locally on your device. They are not held in a Mind the Cards account and may not move to another device.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open a purchase screen and select Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple or Google account that bought the upgrade.

The app will not start or something looks wrong

  1. Close and reopen the app.
  2. Restart the device.
  3. Install the latest available version from the App Store or Google Play.
  4. If the problem continues, email support with your platform, device model, app version and the steps that reproduce it.
